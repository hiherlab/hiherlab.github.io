ChemistryVR

Explore chemistry through virtual experiments.

  • VR
  • Laboratory
  • Experiential learning
  • Simulation
  • Learning

The research question

How can virtual laboratories support hands-on chemistry learning where equipment, space and safety constrain practice?

What we explored

ChemistryVR combines laboratory safety training, direct manipulation of virtual apparatus, step-by-step manuals and simulated chemical reactions. The ICVRV 2022 prototype was developed further in a SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 Educator’s Forum paper.

Why this work matters

The project provides a curriculum-aligned environment for rehearsing experimental procedures without handling real chemicals. Its design connects safety preparation, guided practice and visible chemical reactions in one learning environment.
