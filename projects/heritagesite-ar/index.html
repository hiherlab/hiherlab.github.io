The research question

How can visitors engage with a heritage site through exploration and participation?

What we explored

HeritageSite AR brings mobile augmented reality and an exploration game to a Chinese heritage site. The work combines interaction design with evaluation of the visitor experience.

Why this work matters

A starting point for collaboration between heritage expertise, experience design and field research.

Publication

HeritageSite AR: Design and Evaluation of a Mobile Augmented Reality Exploration Game for a Chinese Heritage Site
