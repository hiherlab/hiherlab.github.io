MagicMap

Find your way through virtual museums.

  • VR
  • Museum
  • Navigation

The research question

How can a visitor navigate a complex virtual museum more intuitively?

What we explored

MagicMap explores navigation through a World-in-Miniature map-based interface. It brings the museum layout into a smaller interactive representation to support exploration.

Why this work matters

An example of interaction research grounded in the practical experience of navigating exhibitions.
