Explore a heritage site across time.

  • AR
  • Heritage site
  • Spatial change

The research question

How can people explore the changing spaces of a heritage site over time?

What we explored

ARTimeTravel explores this question through web-based augmented reality serious games. It extends the lab’s work on place-based heritage experiences.

Why this work matters

A further direction for presenting heritage beyond a single moment in time.

Publication

ARTimeTravel: Understanding Spatial Changes in Heritage Sites Over Time through Web-Based Augmented Reality Serious Games

Jiachen Liang, Yue Li, Gengyuan Zeng and Yiping Dong
