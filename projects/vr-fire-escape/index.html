VR Fire Escape

Different roles. Different ways to learn.

  • VR
  • Safety training
  • Collaboration
  • Role-playing
  • Learning

The research question

How do game modes and player roles affect the experience and learning outcomes of fire-escape training?

What we explored

The system pairs a first-person VR actionist with a desktop strategist in collaborative or competitive play. The study compared their experiences and learning outcomes with paper-based instruction.

Why this work matters

Collaboration improved perceived usability and reduced workload. Desktop strategists showed greater learning improvement than VR actionists, while simulation training did not significantly outperform paper instructions.

Publication

Is VR Always a Better Choice? Investigating the Effects of Game Modes and Role-Playing on Fire Escape Simulation Training
