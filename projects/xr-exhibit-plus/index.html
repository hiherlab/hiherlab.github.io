XR Exhibit+

Make connections through museum conversation.

  • AI
  • Museum
  • Learning
  • VR
  • AR
  • Conversation

The research question

How can an XR museum guide support visitors in constructing meaning and connecting knowledge?

What we explored

XR Exhibit+ combines exhibit tooltips, an LLM conversation interface, a visitor message board and a semantic exhibit graph. A mixed-method study with 28 participants examined AR and VR guides.

Why this work matters

The design connects questions, personal interpretations and related exhibits to support active museum learning.

Publication

XR Exhibit+: An AI-Enhanced XR Museum Guide based on Constructivism and Connectivism Theories
