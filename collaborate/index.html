HER LAB / Collaborate

Let’s ask better questions, together.

For museums, cultural organisations, industry partners and academic collaborators.

Where we can work together

We connect cultural and educational questions with human-centred design and interactive technologies. A collaboration can begin with a collection, a place, a learning challenge or a question about people’s experience.

  • Heritage and museum experiences: explore on-site AR, digital artifacts, and new ways to interpret cultural resources.
  • Learning and play: investigate tangible interfaces and playful approaches to cultural learning.
  • XR interaction and evaluation: study how people navigate, view, interact and engage in virtual or augmented environments.

What a collaboration can involve

We can help define research questions, co-design experiences, develop research prototypes, and evaluate their use. Your domain knowledge, cultural resources and access to real settings can shape the work from the beginning.

The scope, roles, access to materials and intended outcomes are agreed for each project. A useful first step is a focused conversation about the question you want to explore.

See the work

  • ARTimeTravel: web-based AR serious games for understanding spatial changes at heritage sites.
  • CubeMuseum AR: tangible interaction for cultural learning and museum gifting.
  • ResponsiveView: adaptive viewing in virtual museums.
